Set The Record Straight

Myths vs. Facts About Men's Health

Myth: ED only happens to older men.

Fact: It can affect men in their 20s and 30s too, often linked to stress, lifestyle, or an underlying condition — early evaluation catches it faster.

Myth: Premature ejaculation isn't a real medical issue.

Fact: It's one of the most common, treatable concerns we see, usually improved with behavioral techniques and medical guidance together.

Myth: Kidney stones always need surgery.

Fact: Most stones pass on their own with fluids, medication, and monitoring — surgery is reserved for larger or recurring stones.

Myth: Low testosterone is just aging — nothing to fix.

Fact: A simple blood test confirms it, and the right treatment plan can meaningfully improve energy, mood, and performance.

Myth: Adult circumcision means weeks of painful recovery.

Fact: Modern techniques are minimally invasive, and most men resume daily activities within a couple of weeks.
